## 1.2

### Fixes
- Launchers use one round per shot instead of two
- Damage goes through the system so ablated armor, crits, shields and rubber work correctly
- A second incendiary no longer puts out a burning target
- EMP uses the system's own electronics and hardening rules
- Each throw only affects its own template
- Module styles no longer change other dice or cards

### New
- Blast template follows the cursor and shows range and DV
- Throw check rolls Athletics or the launcher skill against the range DV
- One resistance dialog per blast with sheet links, PC and NPC colors, pass and fail boxes and Roll All
- Teargas marks targets with cybereyes as immune
- One summary card for resistance results
- Smoke and special effects can be cleared from the card
- Rave Grenade works with free or Patreon JB2A
- Correct DV tables for thrown grenades, grenade launchers and rock
